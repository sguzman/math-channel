## Acceptance

- All shots render, MP4 decodes completely, correct aspect and frame rate.
- Graph endpoints and point angle correspond; helix geometry represents two turns; final identity is correct.
- Formula legibility, safe margins, consistent colors and intentional motion.
- No abrupt hard cuts; transitions clearly smoother than Example 0001.
- Human quality review of actual footage, including cinematic appeal.
- No narration/music or permanent branding chosen in this trial.
