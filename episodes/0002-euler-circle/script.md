# Optional narration draft

A circle is hiding inside an exponential.

As the real angle t changes, the complex number e to the power i t travels around the unit circle.

Its horizontal and vertical coordinates are cosine and sine. Watch the two waves emerge from the same motion.

Euler's formula brings these ideas together: the complex exponential equals cosine plus i times sine.

Let time become a third dimension, and the rotation becomes a helix.

At pi, the point lands at minus one. Add one, and we arrive at Euler's identity.

One identity. A whole circle of ideas.
